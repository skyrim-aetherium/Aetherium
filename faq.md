### 🔵 How to make the game easier/faster ?

- [**Experience**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/17751) and [**Leveling Freedom**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/69589) both have MCM to customize leveling how you want
- [**Simply Balanced**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/15541) - Has a MCM with many options
- **Remove** [**LESS - Loot Economy Scarcity System**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/182855) - This mod reduces loot quantity
- [**Skyrim Souls RE - Unpaused Menus**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/27859) - Can be disabled or tweaked within it's config file `SkyrimSoulsRE.ini`
- **Disable survival** - Disable **SunHelm** in MCM
- **Injuries** - The injury feature introduced by Blade and Blunt can be toggled in the config file: `BladeAndBlunt.ini`
- [**Apothecary - Vanilla Potion Restore**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/78230) - This mod can restore the vanilla behavior (revert heal over time to instant) - **not tested**
- **Economy** - Tweak [**Trade & Barter**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/23081)'s MCM

### 🔵 How to make the game harder/slower ?

- [**Experience**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/17751) and [**Leveling Freedom**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/69589) both have MCM to customize leveling how you want
- [**Simply Balanced**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/15541) - Has a MCM with many options
- [**LESS - Loot Economy Scarcity System**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/182855) - Harder options available in the FOMOD
- [**SunHelm**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/39414) - cold needs can be enabled in MCM
- [**Ultimate Animated Potions NG**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/97674) - Enable player animation in `UAPNG.ini` file. Use with [**Ultimate Animated Potions NG - ElSopa**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/97891)
- [**Skyrim Souls RE - Unpaused Menus**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/27859) - Tweak to your liking the config file `SkyrimSoulsRE.ini`
- [**Time Flies**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/39426) - Every action takes time (crafting, reading, learning a spell, talking to NPC, etc...)
- [**Know Your Enemy**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/93258) and it's [**Bestiary patch**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/124145) - Adds many strengths and weaknesses to the game
- **Economy** - Tweak [**Trade & Barter**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/23081)'s MCM

### 🔵 Can I add more mods ?

Of course ! You can add and remove any mod, it's all yours. Here is a short list of categories I strongly suggest **not** to expand further to avoid conflicts :

➡️ NPC AI, combat AI, vampire, werewolf, follower framework (NFF), daedric quests, guilds quests and main quest.

> ⚠️ **Skyrim Aetherium** comes with pre-generated assets for **Nemesis**, **DynDOLOD** and **Synthesis**

Installing the following types of mods might requires to manually rerun those patcher on your side:

- Adding new **textures and mesh** for landscape, architecture and flora requires to rerun **DynDOLOD**
- Adding new **enemies, creatures**, **npc** or **quest** requires to rerun **Synthesis patcher** (see [**Aetherium SOAP**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/186196) for detailed list of required patches).
- Adding new **animations**: some requires to rerun **Nemesis** (check on their mod description page)

### 🔵 Does this collection works for Skyrim Anniversary Edition ?

The collection was built and tested **exclusively** on **Skyrim Special Edition**. Optional mods are included to provide compatibility with Creation Club content introduced by the Anniversary Edition, but these have not been tested.

> ℹ️ The worst you can expect when using **Skyrim AE** with **CC content enabled** is minor inconsistencies or visual glitches affecting Creation Club content only.

### 🔵 How to enable nude bodies ?

Nude bodies are disabled by default, if you want to enable them you can reinstall **CBBE** and **Tempered Skins for Males** in Vortex and select your preferred body shapes from the FOMOD installer

### 🔵 Why are ennemies attacking out of range ?

You can add a buffer to enemy attack range in the MCM of [Precision](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/169711) under **miscellaneous** > **AI weapon reach offset**.

### 🔵 Why is my displayed equipment clipping ?

Equipped and favorited equipment are dynamically displayed with [Immersive Equipment Displays](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/62001). You can open IED menu with `Return` and customise everything.

### 🔵 Why is my game so dark/bright ?

**Skyrim Aetherium** aims for an immersive and realistic lighting while maintaining minimal visibility. See [Brightness & Lighting](https://github.com/skyrim-aetherium/Aetherium/blob/main/mods_configuration.md#brightness) for tips.&#x20;

### 🔵 Why is the "Makeup" tab empty in character creation ?

**RaceMenu** loading is inconsistent and sometime the character creation menu shows up before every option is properly loaded. If that happens, create your character as usual and either use the **Shrine of Mara** or console `showracemenu` to show again the **RaceMenu** with every field properly loaded.

### 🔵 How to disable the runes when casting magic ?

- For the runes on the hands: disable [Strange Runes](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/19456),[ Strange Runes Scale options](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/147832) and [Alternative runes - Strange runes](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/137611)
- For the runes around the arm: disable [Runic Arts - Integrated](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/171939) (or use the smaller version)

### 🔵 What about other versions of the game?

**Skyrim Aetherium** is build for version **1.6.1170** and works out of the box with the latest steam version (1.7.104) thanks to [**Skyrim Runtime Swapper**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/189855).

Version **1.6.1170** should work if you disable [**Skyrim Runtime Swapper**](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/189855) (not tested)

The collection won't work for any older version.

I will update the collection to the latest Steam version when possible, many mods still need to by updated by their authors to the latest Bethesda update (1.7.x)
