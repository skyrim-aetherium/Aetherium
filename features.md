# 🎨 Visual
- **Optimized & beautiful** - Skyrim is prettier than ever with even better performances thanks to **Community Shaders** and **NAT.CS III**
- **High definition** textures with high poly meshes and parallax by **Pfuscher's Skyrim 202X**
- **Dynamic lights**, shadows and shaders with **Lux** and **EmbersXD**
- **Flora & Trees** overhauled with Nature of the Wild Lands and Folkvangr
- E**nhanced character creation** - More possibilities, haircuts, face paints, beards, ...
- **Added warpaints** - Available under "Makeup" tab in character creation
- **Modern animations** (1st and 3rd person) for both the Player and NPCs
- **Physics** for clothing, capes, hair and tails
- **Complete NPCs makeover** - Lore-friendly overhaul of every races and faces
- **JK's Skyrim** enhances every major town and place
- **Katanas** & **Spears** reanimated
- **New VFX** for spells, flames and dragon breath


# ⚔️ Gameplay
- Built Around **Simonrim** with cherry-picked content from **Enaisiaion** and **colinswrath**
- **Combat overhaul** - Engaging, balanced and tense combat thanks to Precision, Blade & Blunt, Sanguine Symphony, Dismemberment Framework and completely remade animations
- **Reimagined progression** with **Experience** - Skill leveling and character leveling are independent : progression is achieved through quests and exploration (see Gameplay Overview below) 
- **Combat Stances** (optional) - 4 new combat stances from Stances NG for every melee weapons. They only change animations but allow more depth to combat and adaptation to environment (thrust attacks in tunnels, horizontal sweeps against multiple enemies, etc...). And it looks cool
- **Quickstep dodges** - For you and some roguish NPCs
- **Rebalanced Stealth** - Realistic AI Detection makes light and noise more decisive
- **Rebalance of Combat**, stealth, magic, crafting and cooking - everything useful and effective (even wards !)
- **Spears** - With fully integrated animations, perks compatibility (2 handed swords) and NPC distribution
- **Scrolls & Staff crafting**
- **New Spells** - Vanilla magic has been improved while staying true to Skyrim's spirit with Mysticism and Lost Grimoires
- **Ward spells redone** - Rebalanced cost and effectiveness + wards now block melee attacks and arrows 
- **New Skills and Perks** - **Stormcrown** dragonborn perks, **Hand to Hand** brawler perks, **Security** perks (merged pickpocket and lockpicking)
- **New Bard mechanics** - Skyrim's Got Talent: play instruments, improve and unlock new skills in Speech to provide buffs for you and your followers
- **Vampire overhaul** - **Sacrilege** from **Enairim**. You can avoid sun penalties by covering your head
- **Werewolf overhaul** - **Manbeast** from **Simonrim**
- **Cooking overhaul** - **Gourmet** from **Simonrim**. Meals provide diverse buffs
- **Horses overhaul** - Better control and animations with HorsePower
- **Grab and Throw** any item to distract ennemies or just for fun
- You can jump WHILE sprinting - Be wary, this is incredibly satisfying. You will jump around a lot
- **Nether Follower Framewor**k - Multiple followers, custom dynamic outfits, custom player home, ...
- **QoL** improvements to **Hearthfire**

# 🗺️ Immersion
- **Alternate start** + **Player Origin Story** - More roleplaying possibilities for your character and alternatives to the Helgen start
- **Dynamic animations** for both the player and NPCs depending on equipment, weather, skills, mood and much more
- **Improved NPC AI** on the daily life: towns are alive and NPC interact with each other and react to the world around them - without script bloat
- **Quests expansions** - Many vanilla quests have been expanded and have alternatives. You can finally play as a good guy: refuse to deal with daedras with alternate quests endings, join the Penitus Oculatus and fight the dark brotherhood, and much more...
- **Better pacing** of main quests - You won't become Archmage in 3 days, but you won't either need to complete a thousand radiant quests. Again: balance and fun
- **Many new events** - NPCs travel, factions clash, vigilants of Stendar and Silver Hand can hunt you, ...
- **More conversations & dialogue** lines for everyone - Created with spliced up vanilla lines from More to Say and Jayserpa's amazing dialogue Lines Expansion series
- **Missives boards** - For your classic RPG fetch and deliver quests
- **Landscape and Environment enhanced** - Many new unmarked locations, objects, flora and environmental storytelling added along the roads and into the wild
- **Improved Wildlife** - Many more wild creatures and animals
- **The world evolves** with or without you: some places will be reclaimed by settlers or factions after you clean them
- **Loot & Economy reworked** - You will find less loot and merchants will be tougher negotiators
- **Sunhelm Survival** (optional) - You will need to eat, drink and sleep
- **Easy Camping** - Craft and Buy bedrolls, tents and cooking pots which you can place anywhere. You can even add a fast travel map marker to your camp 
- **Injury system** - When badly hurt in combat, you will suffer small debuffs from injuries. They can be healed with divine blessings and with a good night of sleep
- **Reputation system** - Your actions have consequences and people will react accordingly
- **Artisans** - You can pay NPCs to craft and enchant items for you with Honed Metal
- **Better city guards** - Random City Bags Checks and Suspicious City Guards may investigate (being a Khajiit or an Argonian is considered suspicious in Skyrim...)
- **C.O.I.N** adds new currencies to dungeons loot. Imperial septims shouldn't be found deep within Dwemer ruins or Draugr crypts that have remained untouched for hundreds of years. You can trade ancient coins to Septims from any merchant
- **Hearthfire** and housing **QoL** - Buy materials from your steward, better display cases, ...
- **Better Grabbing** - Allow you to precisely move and rotate object to decorate your home and annoy NPC
